Who can attend?

Are you serious? If you want to make a donation, we would certainly be happy to accept it—but that was just a joke! Everything is free. You simply need to fill out the form and show up at the designated location and time.

You simply need to be in good physical health to participate in the full series of events—oh, and you might want a camera to document the fun moments along the way.

To be honest... I would be sad if you decided not to attend, but we fully respect your decision—and of course, you can cancel before the event starts.
